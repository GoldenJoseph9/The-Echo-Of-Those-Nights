The Echo of Those Nights

We talked for hours, until the night grew old,
And in your voice I heard a gentle "no."

You said you're not in a season for this,
And I understand—I wanted you to know.

The night was cold, the words were kind,
And nothing harsh was left behind.

But some things can't be held too tight,
So I'll let the echo fade into the night.

I wanted you—I won't pretend,
But I can't force a heart, or make a season bend.

I watched you speak, I heard you clear,
And still I wished the words were different here.

I memorized the way you laughed,
A sound I'd keep if I could keep just half.

But I can't be the friend who stands aside,
With all this feeling I have to hide.

So here's my hand, and here's my grace,
I'll hold the good, and leave the rest in place.

May your season come, may your heart find spring,
And may you find the risk is worth the everything.

And as for me—I'll find my way,
To someone who'll choose to stay.

— with warmth, Gabriel
